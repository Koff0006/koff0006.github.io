Life of a HUSKY

The life of a husky is full of energy and adventure.

Huskies are friendly and hardworking dogs that were originally bred from the snowy landscape.

They love running,playing and exploring the outdoors, they need exercise to stay healthy and happy.

They also love to spend time with their owners and other dogs.

They bring joy and excitement at any home and living with a husky means sharing an active lifestyle and building a loyal friendship

Facts About Huskies

  • Siberian Huskies were originally bred by the Chukchi people in Siberia to pull sleds over long distances in very cold weather.
  • Huskies need a lot of exercise and activity. If they get bored, they may start digging, chewing, or trying to escape.
  • Many Huskies have blue eyes, but some have brown eyes, and some even have one blue and one brown eye. This is called heterochromia.
